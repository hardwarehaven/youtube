
# Sponsors
*updated: 11-27-2026*

This is a list of all of the sponsors that I've worked with in the past. I've detailed whether or not I use the product/service, as well as any other notes, such as why I might have stopped working with the sponsor.

> [!note] 
> If you think that I've missed a sponsor or that a correction needs to be made, don't hesitate to reach out. Just send me an email: info@hardwarehaven.media

## List of Sponsors
### AMP game servers:
**do I use it:** Yes! I don't have much time for multiplayer gaming, but I've kept it running in a VM for when it's needed. Just recently, my wife and I started playing Enshrouded together, and spinning up a server with AMP was super easy.

### Antlion Audio:
**do I use it:** Yep! I use thier USB mod mic all the time. 

### Aranet:
**do I use it:** I received an aranet4 for an integrated advertisement and it currently is in use in my office. I haven’t yet had a need to buy another. Also, I've found that a few years later there are multiple other options on the market, many of which integrate better with something like home assistant and are more affordable. 

### Aura:
**do I use it:** No. I used it for a couple of years or so, but found that it wasn't worth it since I was already paying for a separate VPN and password manager. I also don't trust in the data broker removal industry as a whole. 

### Bambu Lab:
**do I use it:** Yes, I use my A1 mini and X1CC all the time. I was sent these for free as part of bambu lab’s partner program. I was a fan of the company for quite some time, and I believe they make very high quality products. Their policies regarding privacy and open source software have led me to no longer make public recommendations of their products. 

### BenQ:
**do I use it:** I absolutely love the RD280UG monitors they sent for an advertisement, and I've considered buying on of the 60Hz models for the studio space.

### boot.dev:
**do I use it:** Not anymore, but that's just because it's not a priority for me. I thought the service was pretty neat and useful.

### Factor:
**do I use it:** Off and on. It can be a bit expensive, but also useful when I need somewhat healthy meals on a busy schedule. 

### Fanttik:
**do I use it:** Yep! I love some of their tools, including the S2 Pro and B10 Pro Max. They get used a ton.

### Flexispot
**do I use it:** Yes, I have like 5 flexispot desks at this point, many of which I have bought personally. I also use two of their chairs, one of which I bought personally. 

### Hellofresh:
**do I use it:** Not really. We used it for a while, but found it less useful after having a picky toddler. 

### jawa.gg:
**do I use it:** Yes! I've found a lot of great deals on there. 

### Libernovo:
**do I use it:** Yes, their Omni Pro is my main office chair and I love it. They did send that for free for the sponsorship. I turned down libernovo for a while, as reviews of the original omni weren't great. However, it seemed that they made a lot of improvements based on user/reviewer feedback for the Imni Pro and Omni SE. The Pro is probably too expensive for my tastes, but I would absolutely consider buying the SE if I needed another office chair. 

### LMG/LTT Store/Creator Warehouse:
**do I use it:** I use quite a few of their products. The LTT screwdriver is by far the best ratcheting screwdriver I've ever used—I've bought some of these to give away as gifts. The precision screwdriver is also fantastic. I received both of those for free, but have bought a variety of other products from lttstore, including other screwdrivers, clothing, the kids book, cable ties, and probably other things.

### Magic Mind
**do I use it:** Not currently. I liked it but it is a bit too expensive for me.

### Magic Spoon
**do I use it:** Not currently, no. I will say that I typically have trouble with sugar free and similar alternative products, but I found magic spoon to taste a lot better to me. That being said, my wife isn’t a fan. We don’t eat a ton of cereal, so it just isn’t worth buying magic spoon just for me.

### Manscaped
**do I use it:** Yep. I love the beard trimmer and weed whacker and use them both in my regular routine. I won’t go into too much detail about the lawn mower lineup, but I do recommend them. I received most of my manscaped products for free for use in integrations, but I pay for replacement blades when needed.

### Manta Sleep:
**do I use it:** My wife does. She uses hers every night and loves it. I bought myself one, and use it occasionally for naps. 

### Monarch (previously Monarch Money)
**do I use it:** At the time of writing this, yes. I did find the service to work really well, and I wish that I could continue working with them. However, I found that they had a history of advertising that they "don't sell your data", while going that. They've since updated marketing materials to explicitly say that they won't sell your "financial data". Moving forward, I will decline any partnerships as well as cancel my own subscription.  

### Nordpass
**do I use it:** No. I didn’t have issues with the service, but I’m not a fan of some of Nord’s marketing practices, so I won’t be working with them unless those are fixed. 

### Notion
**do I use it:** Yep! I still use notion to keep track of all my video projects as well as personal notes.

### Odoo:
**do I use it:** No, but if I had a larger company I would consider it. 

### OnShape:
**do I use it:** All the time. They have provided me with th pro membership as part of advertisements, but I don't really use it much. I'm fine with the free public tier, as I don't really care if other people can see my designs. I try to share most of the good ones anyway. 

### Private  Internet Access:
**do I use it:** Yes. I primarily use it with a torrent stack that I run. It's been great for that. I also use it on when I occasionally need a VPN server somewhere out of the country. I've never had any issues as a customer. I've stopped working with them, primarily just because I've decided to cut back on advertising subscription services. 

### Server Part Deals:
**do I use it:** I wish. RIP hard drive prices... In all seriousness, SPD is fantastic and it's been the place that I not only buy most of my HDDs from, but it's also my recommendation for others. Unfortunately the current hard drive market isn't great. 

### Skillshare
**do I use it:** I did when I had a free trial. I liked it at first, but found that while some courses were great, many were fairly low quality, and the topics were somewhat limited. Because I don’t care for the service after spending more time with it, I won’t be working with Skillshare in the future.

### Southern New Hampshire University
**do I use it:** Well no, I don’t plan to go back to college any time soon. 

### Squarespace
**do I use it:** Yes, I use squarespace for my website because it’s simple and easy. I was offered a free trial for the advertisements, but had already been paying before that. I also use it for my domain name management. 

### Storyblocks:
**do I use it:** Yes, whenever I need random b-roll, sound effects, or music.

### Trade Coffee:
**do I use it:** Yep. It's possibly a little biy overpriced vs getting something locally, but it's nice to not have to think about it. The coffee selections are great!

### UGREEN
**do I use it:** I’ve used a lot of various UGREEN products. I've been sent many accessories for integrated advertisements, as well as NAS products for review. I've also bought many cables, chargers, and accessories personally. For example, I bought one of their high end KVM switches for my desk, as it was the most affordable options for dual 4K 120HZ monitors, and it works great!

### World of Warships
**do I use it:** No, but to be fair I barely have time to play games. I found it to be somewhat fun though, especially with it being free to play.
